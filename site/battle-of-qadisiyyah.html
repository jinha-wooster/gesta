Prologue
Grounded in al-Tabari's History of the Prophets and Kings, al-Baladhuri's Futuh al-Buldan, and the fragments of Sayf ibn 'Umar preserved in later sources. Arab strength estimated at 30,000-36,000; Sassanid at 100,000-120,000 with 33 war elephants. Each figure represents roughly 1,500 men.
