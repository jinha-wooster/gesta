Prologue
Grounded in Polybius, who spoke with men who fought here, and Livy, who wrote the parley into Latin for the ages. Perhaps 40,000 Carthaginians with 80 war elephants against 35,000 Romans and the Numidian cavalry that once served Carthage. One figure represents roughly 500 men, or 8 elephants.
