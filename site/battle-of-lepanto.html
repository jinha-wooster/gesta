Sources: Gian Pietro Contarini's contemporary account, Fernand Braudel's The Mediterranean, and John F. Guilmartin's Gunpowder and Galleys. The Holy League fielded roughly 206 galleys, 6 galleasses, and some 84,000 men; the Ottomans roughly 251 galleys, 56 galliots, and 88,000 men. One figure represents approximately 3 galleys or 1,200 oarsmen and soldiers.
