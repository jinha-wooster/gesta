Prologue
Grounded in the Swedish military journal, Theatrum Europaeum, and Tilly's own dispatches. The Imperial-Catholic League army numbered approximately 35,000 under Field Marshal Tilly; the Swedish-Saxon force totalled roughly 42,000, of whom the Saxons (c.18,000) broke early. Swedish salvo casualties at Breitenfeld have been estimated at 7,000 Imperial dead and 6,000 captured. Swedish losses: approximately 2,100 killed and wounded. One figure represents approximately 1,000 men.
