Prologue
Grounded in Yi Sun-sin's diary (Nanjung Ilgi) and the Joseon Wangjo Sillok. Approximately 56 Korean warships under Yi Sun-sin, Yi Ok-gi, and Won Gyun against 73 Japanese vessels under Wakisaka Yasuharu. Japanese forces lost roughly 59 ships sunk or captured; Korean losses were minimal. One figure represents roughly 2 ships.
