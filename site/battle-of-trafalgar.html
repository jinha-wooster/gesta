Prologue
Grounded in the dispatches of Vice-Admiral Collingwood, the log of HMS Victory, and the account of Surgeon Beatty who attended Nelson in the cockpit. Casualties: British 458 killed, 1,208 wounded; Franco-Spanish fleet approximately 3,243 killed, 2,538 wounded, 7,000 captured, 22 ships taken or destroyed. Nelson died at 4:30 p.m., learning the fleet had taken fourteen prizes. One figure represents roughly 1 ship of the line and her crew (MEN_PER = 1).
