Prologue
Grounded in Caesar's Gallic War VII, with the caveat that the main source is the victor's own memoir. His quarter-million relief army is rejected by modern historians (likely 80,000 to 100,000); losses on both sides are uncertain. The double fortification is not legend: Napoleon III's excavations at Alise-Sainte-Reine found the ditches, the traps, and the towers where Caesar said they were. One figure represents roughly 200 to 500 men.
