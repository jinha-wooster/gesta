Grounded in al-Baladhuri's Futuh al-Buldan, al-Waqidi, Theophanes the Confessor, and Walter Kaegi's Byzantium and the Early Islamic Conquests (1992). Casualty figures are among the most disputed in early Islamic history: estimates range from tens of thousands to six figures on the Byzantine side. One figure represents roughly 500 men.
