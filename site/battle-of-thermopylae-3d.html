Prologue
Grounded in Herodotus, Histories VII, and mainstream scholarship. Herodotus's Persian millions are rejected by modern historians (likely 70,000 to 300,000); the role of the Thebans and the exact ground are debated. Where accounts differ, this simulation follows the most widely accepted reconstruction. One figure represents roughly 25 to 40 men.
