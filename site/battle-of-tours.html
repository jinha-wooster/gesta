Prologue
Primary sources include the Continuations of Fredegar, the Chronicle of 754 (written in Iberia within a generation), and Isidore of Beja. Modern historians disagree on army sizes: the Umayyad force was likely 20,000-80,000; the Frankish force perhaps 15,000-30,000. One figure represents roughly 400 men.
