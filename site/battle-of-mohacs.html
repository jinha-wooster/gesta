Prologue
Grounded in Suleiman's own campaign diary, the chronicle of Kemalpashazade, and the accounts of Croatian diplomat and eyewitness Stefan Brodarics, who survived the battle. Each figure represents roughly 1,000 men. Ottoman strength estimated at 50,000-60,000; Hungarian at 25,000-26,000.
