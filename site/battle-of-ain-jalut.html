Prologue
Grounded in Ibn Shaddad, al-Maqrizi, and Rashid al-Din. Estimated forces: roughly 12,000 Mamluks under Qutuz and Baibars against a Mongol column of 10,000 to 20,000 under Kitbuqa Noyan. Hulagu had withdrawn the main Mongol force to Persia; what met the Mamluks was a powerful but not overwhelming detachment. One figure represents roughly 500 men.
