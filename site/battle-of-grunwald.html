Prologue
Grounded in Jan Dlugosz, whose Annales recorded the battle within a generation of the men who fought it, and the anonymous Cronica conflictus written within months of the day itself. Estimated forces: 27,000 to 39,000 Polish-Lithuanian, 17,000 to 27,000 Teutonic Order. One figure represents roughly 500 men.
