Prologue
Grounded in the Gesta Henrici Quinti, written by a priest who watched from the baggage line, and the French chronicles of Monstrelet and Waurin. Numbers are argued to this day: tradition says the French outnumbered the English five to one, careful modern counting says perhaps two to one, and the mud did not care either way. One figure represents roughly 50 men.
