Prologue
Grounded in the Anglo-Saxon Chronicle, Orderic Vitalis, William of Poitiers, and the Bayeux Tapestry, which records the day in stitches if not always in agreement. Harold brought perhaps 7,000 men to the ridge; William perhaps 7,500 to 8,000 across all arms. One figure represents roughly 500 men.
