Prologue
Grounded in the Novgorod First Chronicle, the Galician-Volhynian Chronicle, and the Persian and Chinese accounts of the great raid. Numbers are contested: perhaps 20,000 Mongols against a coalition somewhere between 30,000 and twice that, depending on which frightened chronicler is counting. One figure represents roughly 300 men.
