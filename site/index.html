How a battle is made Every title in this catalogue is compiled from the same grammar: a fixed historical spine, two to four real decisions rendered as choices and counterfactuals you can rewind, one or two command beats where the field itself is in your hands, and an aftermath that tells the truth about what it all changed. One engine, one design language, a new decisive hour every week. Story in, experience out.
