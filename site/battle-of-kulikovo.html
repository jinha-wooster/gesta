Grounded in the Zadonshchina, the Chronicle of the Battle of Kulikovo, and the Life of Sergius of Radonezh. Casualty estimates vary widely: modern scholarship suggests 5,000 to 15,000 Russian dead; period sources cite figures far higher. Tatar losses are unrecorded. One figure represents roughly 600 men.
