Primary sources: Doukas, Kritovoulos, Sphrantzes, Leonard of Chios, and Nicolo Barbaro's diary written inside the walls. The siege ran fifty-three days: 6 April to 29 May 1453. Approximately 7,000 defenders held four miles of wall against a force estimated between 50,000 and 80,000. One figure represents approximately 250 men.
