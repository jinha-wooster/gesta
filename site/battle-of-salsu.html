Prologue
Grounded in the Samguk Sagi (thirteenth century compilation of earlier records) and the Book of Sui. The chronicles record 2,700 survivors from the 305,000 who crossed the Cheongcheon: a figure so extreme that historians debate the original army's true size rather than dismissing the ratio. The poem Eulji sent Yu Zhongwen is the oldest surviving Korean literary work in Chinese script. One figure represents roughly 500 men.
