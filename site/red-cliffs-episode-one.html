Prologue
Grounded in the Records of the Three Kingdoms and Sima Guang's history, and carefully separated from the Romance of the Three Kingdoms, the 14th-century novel that made this the most famous battle in Chinese history and invented many of its best moments. Numbers, the site itself, and who chained the ships are all debated. One model ship stands for a squadron; one figure is roughly 500 men. Old Gan, Shen Lan, and Zhao Tu are inventions in the manner of historical fiction; the fleet, the wind, and the fire are not.
