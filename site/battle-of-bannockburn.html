Prologue
Grounded in John Barbour's The Brus (c. 1375), the Lanercost Chronicle, and Vita Edwardi Secundi. Scottish forces: roughly 6,000 to 8,000 spearmen and a small mounted contingent. English forces: estimates range from 15,000 to 20,000, including heavy cavalry and longbowmen. One figure represents roughly 500 men.
