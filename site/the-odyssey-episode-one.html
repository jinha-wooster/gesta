The landfall
Adapted from Book 9 of Homer's Odyssey, composed in the oral tradition around the 8th century BC and told by Odysseus himself at a Phaeacian banquet, which is to say by a narrator with every reason to improve the story. Polites, Eurylochus, and Elpenor are Homer's own crewmen, here given the lines the poem never wrote down.
