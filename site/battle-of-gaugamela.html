Prologue
Grounded in Arrian's Anabasis, drawn from eyewitness accounts via Ptolemy and Aristobulus, and checked against Diodorus and Curtius Rufus. Casualty figures are ancient estimates and contested: perhaps 300,000 Persian dead and wounded against fewer than 500 Macedonians, though modern historians treat these numbers with caution. One figure represents roughly 1,000 men.
