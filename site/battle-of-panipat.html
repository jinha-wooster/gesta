Prologue
Grounded in the Baburnama, Babur's own account written within weeks of the battle: the most detailed first-person record of any pre-modern commander in the field. Ibrahim Lodi's host numbered perhaps 100,000 with up to 1,000 war elephants; Babur brought roughly 12,000 to 15,000 veterans from Kabul. One figure represents approximately 1,000 men.
