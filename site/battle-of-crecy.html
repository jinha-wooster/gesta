Prologue
Grounded in Jean Froissart's Chronicles, compiled within a generation from men who were there. Philip VI's own heralds counted 1,542 knights and esquires dead on the field; estimates of total French losses range from 1,200 to 4,000, against perhaps 200 to 300 English. The blind King John of Bohemia died here asking his knights to lead him close enough to strike one blow: they tied their horses together and rode into the press. One figure represents roughly 300 men.
