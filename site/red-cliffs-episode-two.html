The flight
Adapted from chapters 49-50 of Luo Guanzhong's Romance of the Three Kingdoms (c. 14th century), which dramatizes the historical escape of Cao Cao after the defeat at Red Cliffs in winter 208 CE. The three laughs, the ambushes, and Guan Yu's act of mercy at Huarong are the novel's invention; the defeat, the escape route through Wulin, and the survival of Cao Cao are historical. Cheng Yu, Zhang Liao, and Guan Yu are historical figures; the lines given to them here are imagined from what the sources and the novel imply. Figure scale: each soldier figure represents approximately 500 men. The catalogue does not look away from what commands cost, or who pays them.
