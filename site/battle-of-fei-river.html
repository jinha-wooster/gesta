Prologue
Grounded in the Jin Shu (Book of Jin) and Zizhi Tongjian (Comprehensive Mirror in Aid of Governance), compiled by Sima Guang. The Qin army is recorded at 870,000 to one million; modern estimates set the engaged force at roughly 250,000. The Jin fielded some 80,000. One figure represents approximately 10,000 men.
