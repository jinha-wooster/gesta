Prologue
Grounded in the Tang Huiyao, the Zizhi Tongjian of Sima Guang, and the accounts preserved in al-Tabari and Ibn al-Athir. Modern analysis by Christopher Beckwith, H.A.R. Gibb, and Sven Beckwith informs the Karluk sequence and force estimates. Tang strength circa 20,000 to 30,000, Abbasid and allied forces larger. Heavy Tang losses; Arab sources record skilled Chinese prisoners, including papermakers, taken to Samarkand. One figure represents roughly 300 men.
