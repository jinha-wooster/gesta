Prologue
Grounded in Herodotus (Histories, Book 8), who gathered testimony a generation after the battle, and Aeschylus, who fought at Marathon and wrote his Persians for an Athenian audience of veterans eight years later. Greek fleet: approximately 310 to 380 triremes from seventeen allied city-states. Persian fleet: 400 to 600, after storm losses at Artemisium, drawn from Phoenicia, Ionia, Egypt, and Cilicia. One figure represents approximately 8 ships.
